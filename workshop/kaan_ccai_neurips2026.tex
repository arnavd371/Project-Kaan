\begin{abstract}
Post-harvest insect damage is a major food-security and climate-relevant loss pathway in stored grain.
We present \textbf{Kaan}, an open, offline smartphone system that classifies contact acoustics into clean grain versus three primary stored-rice pests using a compact INT8 CNN.
Rather than chasing laboratory accuracy alone, we emphasize deployment constraints: leakage-aware evaluation, multi-approach bake-offs, teacher distillation into a phone-sized model, temperature calibration with selective abstention, and a \emph{robustness ladder} that stress-tests phone-like degradations without new field recordings.
On IRRI acoustic data with ambient clean windows, a distilled production model reaches 97.2\% validation accuracy; multi-seed bake-offs show classical and deep models both exceed a cited 84.51\% reference under our protocol.
The robustness ladder reveals sharp drops under additive noise and telephone band-limiting---a limitation we treat as a first-class result for climate-adaptation tooling that must survive rural handsets.
Code and models are released under Apache-2.0.
\end{abstract}

\section{Climate impact pathway}
Reducing avoidable post-harvest loss lowers pressure to expand production and associated emissions, while improving food availability for smallholders~\cite{igmri2015,worldbank_phl}.
Acoustic screening can catch internal feeders before visual symptoms, enabling earlier aeration, sunning, or fumigation decisions.
Kaan targets \emph{adaptation} (protecting stored food under climate stress on storage conditions) and indirect \emph{mitigation} (less waste).
We deliberately optimize for offline phones: no cloud inference, multilingual advisories, and models small enough for browser/ONNX and TFLite.

\section{Problem and system}
\textbf{Classes:} clean, rice weevil (\emph{Sitophilus oryzae}), lesser grain borer (\emph{Rhyzopertha dominica}), red flour beetle (\emph{Tribolium castaneum}).
\textbf{Pipeline:} 10\,s @ 16\,kHz mono $\rightarrow$ 128$\times$128 mel $\rightarrow$ INT8 CNN $\rightarrow$ confidence gate.
Pest WAVs come from the IRRI Rice Acoustic Sensor dataset~\cite{balingbing2024}; clean uses Speech Commands ambient windows rather than synthetic silence.
Splits are stratified at \emph{file} level after byte-deduplication to avoid train/val twins.

\section{Methods}
\paragraph{Bake-off.}
Eleven approaches (mel-CNNs, 1D CNN, YAMNet probe, SVM/MLP/GBDT/RF/ExtraTrees/kNN/logreg) share one leakage-aware split; we report multi-seed means, bootstrap CIs, and McNemar tests.
\paragraph{Distillation.}
Soft labels from GBDT + ExtraTrees + deep CNN train a deep student for deployment (TFLite $\sim$333\,KB; ONNX for web).
\paragraph{Robustness ladder.}
Without field mics, we replay validation audio under pink SNR, phone band-pass, muffling, compression, clipping, reverb, gain/AGC, and combinations---reporting accuracy per rung.
\paragraph{Calibration.}
Temperature scaling fitted on a validation slice; abstain curves trade coverage for accuracy.
\paragraph{Confusion-focused heads.}
Cost-sensitive upweighting and a fine-tuned weevil$\leftrightarrow$borer specialist on the baseline encoder with \emph{strict} fusion (override only when top-2 are that pair and margin is small; gate swept on val).
\paragraph{SSL.}
SimCLR-style SpecAugment views on IRRI+ambient mels, then supervised fine-tune.

\section{Results}
Table~\ref{tab:bakeoff} summarizes the multi-seed bake-off (reference line: cited 84.51\%~\cite{balingbing2024} under \emph{our} protocol, not a locked reimplementation).
Distillation (seed 42) yields \textbf{97.15\%} val accuracy (macro-F1 0.977) versus 95.57\% hard-only deep and 95.89\% teacher ensemble.
SSL fine-tune reaches 97.78\% on the same seed.
Calibration reduces ECE (e.g., $0.044\rightarrow0.030$ at $T{=}0.5$ on seed 42).

Table~\ref{tab:robust} is the deployment-facing result: laboratory mel-CNNs survive mild muffling but \textbf{fail under phone-band and noise}---exactly the shift rural handsets induce.
From-scratch hierarchical cascades collapse; fine-tuned specialists reach $\sim$97\% on the pair subset alone, and strict gating aims to preserve baseline overall accuracy (multi-seed aggregates in the supplement / released JSON).

\section{Limitations (domain shift)}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{No Indian phone-on-bag corpus yet.} All numbers use IRRI contact sensors plus ambient clean windows. The robustness ladder is a proxy, not a substitute for field labels.
  \item \textbf{Species coverage.} Pulse beetle and other pests are out of scope.
  \item \textbf{Reference comparison} to 84.51\% is soft (protocol-matched claim, not identical reimplementation).
  \item \textbf{Single geography / grain type} in source data; storage bag materials and vernacular mics vary.
  \item \textbf{Screening aid only}---not legal or lab diagnosis; low-confidence UX must abstain.
\end{itemize}

\section{Open release and outlook}
Apache-2.0 code, distilled weights, bake-off tables, robustness/calibration JSON, and a static results dashboard are public.\footnote{Anonymized for review; camera-ready will cite the repository.}
Next: when feasible, phone-mic field collection with extension partners, and tighter phone-matched augmentation.
Ground-up, small-model, offline tooling matches CCAI's call for resource-aware climate ML.
